Plaster wall  (wall)

Wall tile, 64x96: footprint diamond in the bottom 32px, 64px of height. Blocks movement, fades when occluding.

Files:
  tile.png  64x96

_guide_*.png are reference overlays (magenta = footprint diamond, cyan = anchor);
they are never used in-game. Paint over the placeholder PNGs, keep the exact size,
keep everything outside the artwork transparent, then press Test in the Art Pipeline window.
